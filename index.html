一 · The Studio

We remove until it feels inevitable.

Most studios begin with a feature list and negotiate downward. We begin with the smallest version that could work, and only add when playtesting proves the game is worse without it. The result is a catalogue that looks sparse and plays complete.

間 · Space

Interfaces that breathe

Margins are a feature. If an element is not helping you play, it is taken away rather than made prettier.

静 · Quiet

No manufactured pressure

No timers, no streak decay, no countdown offers. Nothing in our games punishes you for putting the phone down.

続 · Continuity

Supported for years

A title from 2020 still receives compatibility work. A release is the start of upkeep, not the end of it.

完成

Tom — a puzzle with no clock and no penalty.

Our flagship release is a hand-authored deduction puzzle. There is no timer, no fail state, and no resource that drains while you think.

Each board introduces a single idea and asks you to combine it with everything the game has taught so far. Hints are free and unlimited, progress is written on every move, and the whole game runs offline.

二 · Principles

Four commitments we do not trade.

壱

No manufactured waiting

Energy meters and cooldowns are not added to games that do not need them. Difficulty belongs in the puzzle, never in the shop.

弐

Price before the tap

Anything purchasable is priced and described before you commit. No invented scarcity, no recurring “limited” offers.

参

Minimal collection

We gather only what is required to run and improve the game, stated plainly in our privacy policy.

四

Fix before feature

A known crash is repaired before new content starts. A broken build is not a roadmap item — it is a broken product.

三 · Contact

Write to the studio

Publishers, partners and players reach the same inbox, and it is read by the people who build the games. Enquiries are answered in the order they arrive.
